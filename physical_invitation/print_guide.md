# Luxury Physical Wedding Invitation Printing Guide
## Mustafa & Tasneem — Dawoodi Bohra Royal Wedding Stationery Suite

This print production guide is prepared for the couple and professional wedding print vendors. It specifies exact dimensions, paper stocks, print finishes, and step-by-step export instructions for:
1. **The International Tri-Fold Gatefold Invitation Card** (Inspired by Instagram `@batulhuzefa`)
2. **The Ceremony Welcome Easel Board** (Inspired by Instagram `@designedbysakinah`)
3. **The Royal Islamic Rosette Medallion Logo** (Inspired by Instagram `@elegant_khatt`)

## 1. Tri-Fold Gatefold Invitation Specifications

### Dimensions & Layout
* **Flat Unfolded Sheet Size:** International A4 Landscape — `297 mm (width) × 210 mm (height)`
* **Folded Finished Size:** `99 mm (width) × 210 mm (height)`
* **Panels:** 3 Panels (`99mm` Left Flap, `99mm` Center Main Panel, `99mm` Right Flap)
* **Crease / Score Lines:**
  * Score 1: at `99 mm` from left edge
  * Score 2: at `198 mm` from left edge
* **Bleed Margin:** `3 mm` on all outer edges (`303 mm × 216 mm` total print area).

### Recommended Paper Stock
* **Paper Type:** Premium Textured Cotton Rag, Tintoretto Gesso, or Stucco Matte Felt Finish.
* **Paper Weight:** `300 GSM` to `350 GSM` (heavyweight rigid luxury feel).
* **Color Base:** Natural Off-White / Ivory Cream with subtle woven or felt texture.

### Recommended Luxury Print Embellishments
* **Gold Foil Stamping (Hot Stamping):**
  * Apply metallic liquid gold foil on the **Islamic Rosette Medallion**, **"Mustafa weds Tasneem"**, the **Bismillah**, and the **Mughal Archway** filigree accents.
* **Embossing / Debossing:**
  * Multi-level blind embossing along the arched gatefold frame to create a tangible 3D relief.
* **Envelope Pairing:**
  * Matching `105 mm × 220 mm` (DL+) envelope in pastel mint sage or soft royal lavender, sealed with a physical gold wax seal with initials `MT` and `م ت`.

## 2. Ceremony Welcome Easel Board Specifications

### Dimensions & Mounting
* **Standard Poster / Easel Size:** `A3 Portrait (297 mm × 420 mm)` or `18 in × 24 in (450 mm × 600 mm)`.
* **Substrate / Mounting:** `5 mm` or `8 mm` Rigid Foam Core Board (Sunboard) or Canvas Wrap on wooden easel.
* **Finish:** Matte Velvet Lamination or Soft-Touch Lamination (anti-reflective under event lighting and photography flash).

## 3. How to Export Print-Ready PDFs from the Project

1. Open the interactive print studio in your browser:
   - Locally: `http://localhost:8099/physical_invitation/index.html`
   - Or open the file: `physical_invitation/index.html` in Chrome / Edge.
2. Personalize the guest name using the top input field:
   - Type any family name (e.g. *"Janab Amil Saheb & Family"* or *"Mufaddal Bhai & Family"*). The front cover will automatically render the name in calligraphy.
3. Click the **"🖨️ Print / Save as PDF"** button (or press `Ctrl + P` / `Cmd + P`).
4. In the print dialog:
   - **Destination:** Save as PDF
   - **Layout:** Landscape (for Tri-fold) or Portrait (for Easel Board)
   - **Paper size:** A4 (or A3 for Easel)
   - **Margins:** None
   - **Options:** Check **"Background graphics"** (Essential to render the botanical background and gold foils).
5. Click **Save** to generate your high-resolution print PDF.
