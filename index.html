Pre-Wedding Sacred Ritual

માણેક થંભ (Manak Thamb)

Auspicious installation of the sacred pillar to welcome barakaat, health, and prosperity for the new household.

🗓️ Friday, 12th December 2026 ⏰ 10:00 AM onwards 📍 Saifee Villa, Malabar Hill, Mumbai

02
Haldi & Canopy Songs

માંડવો અને પીઠી (Mandvo & Peethi)

Blessing the ceremonial canopy with fragrant turmeric, auspicious songs, and traditional Madeh.

🗓️ Friday, 12th December 2026 ⏰ 04:30 PM 📍 Royal Emerald Courtyard, Mumbai
📍 View on Google Maps
03
Family Tradition

મામા મોસાળું અને કંઠી (Mama Mosala & Kanthi)

Ceremonial presentation of festive bridal jewelry, auspicious sweets, and honors from maternal uncles.

🗓️ Saturday, 13th December 2026 ⏰ 06:30 PM 📍 Lavender Garden Terrace, Mumbai
📍 View on Google Maps
04
Spiritual Gathering

دريْس ومجلس (Darees & Majlis)

Recitation of the Holy Quran, zikr, praises, and prayers seeking divine blessings prior to the Nikah.

🗓️ Sunday, 14th December 2026 ⏰ 11:00 AM 📍 Al-Masjid al-Saifee Markaz, Mumbai
📍 View on Google Maps
05
عقد النكاح المبارك

عقد النكاح (Aqd al-Nikah & Zafaf)

Solemnization of marriage under the pious Raza Mubarak of Syedna Mufaddal Saifuddin (TUS), followed by the joyful wedding procession.

🗓️ Sunday, 14th December 2026 ⏰ 01:30 PM Sharp 📍 Grand Saifee Darbar Hall, Mumbai
📍 View on Google Maps
06
Traditional Bohra Feast

ખુશીનું જમણ (Khushi nu Jaman)

Traditional communal feast served in the Thaal, beginning with Namak, Sodanna, Halwa, royal savories & Biryani, ending with paan & ittar.

🗓️ Sunday, 14th December 2026 ⏰ 07:30 PM onwards 📍 The Crystal Ballroom, Grand Hyatt, Mumbai
📍 View on Google Maps
07
Evening Gala

Walima & Reception

An enchanting evening of congratulations, memories, and joy under starry chandeliers.
